Fluid overload sends heart failure and cirrhosis patients back to hospital. We are building a way to see it coming.

Heart failure drives over 1.2 million U.S. hospital admissions a year, at a cost of tens of billions of dollars. Most begin with the same event: fluid overload.

We are building a wearable monitoring platform that measures fluid directly, so millions of heart and liver patients can stay healthy, and stay home.
